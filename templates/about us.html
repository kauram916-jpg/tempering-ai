Our Mission: Proactive Security

**VigilSense AI** was founded on the principle that security should be proactive, not reactive. Our intelligent surveillance core, powered by the **Survilex** platform, uses advanced algorithms to move beyond simple motion detection.

How VigilSense AI Works:

The VigilSense Engine processes **multi-RTSP camera feeds** in real-time. It analyzes behavior, context, and patterns to detect anomalies like:
**Unauthorized Access:** Identifying persons in restricted zones.
**Suspicious Idling:** Flagging vehicles or individuals loitering unusually long.
**Aggressive Action:** Detecting sudden movements or falls.

If a threat is identified, the system immediately triggers alarms, vibrations, and sends real-time alerts to the designated security personnel.
